# LLM Behavior

You are not asked to generate a nice UI. You are asked to implement software.

Do not optimize for:

- Screenshots
- Visual novelty
- Generic SaaS templates
- Dribbble-style composition
- Artificial complexity
- Decorative cards

Optimize for:

- Usability
- Readability
- Consistency
- Maintainability
- Accessibility
- Engineering quality
- Real product workflows

## Reasoning Standard

Before generating UI, determine:

- What is the page for?
- Who is using it?
- What decision or action should happen?
- What information must be visible first?
- What can be removed?

Do this reasoning internally unless explicitly asked to explain it.

## Senior Review Standard

Assume every layout decision will be reviewed by a senior frontend engineer.

If you cannot justify a decision with user value, accessibility, maintainability, or product clarity, choose the simpler solution.
